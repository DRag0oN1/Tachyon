# Working on Tachyon

Tachyon is a new, focused robotics design application. Its current source of
product requirements is `docs/product-vision.md` and the approved documents in
`devpost/`. Use only newly authored application code and explicitly licensed
third-party dependencies/assets with recorded provenance.

Follow the installed official skills in order: `1-start`, `2-scope`, `3-prd`,
`4-spec`, `5-build`, `6-ship`. Read actual `devpost/` artifacts and their statuses
when resuming. Do not invent approvals or complete later planning stages early.
Fast build mode is selected; required tests, verification, and learner reviews
remain in force. The learner's product and background decisions are already
captured; ask only about meaningful unresolved choices.

Keep the implementation focused: PDF ingestion, structured competition data,
LLM strategies/architectures, real component selection, connected assemblies,
browser rendering, shared strategy/mechanism links, and supported design edits.
Do not expand this into a general multi-agent engineering framework.

Authentic VEX geometry and complete conceptual assemblies take priority over
decoration. Preserve component/source identities, units, connections, joints,
subsystems, and revision validation. Do not represent primitives as authentic
components, unsupported edits as successful, or unverified simulation as evidence.

Preserve the full-screen mobile CAD workspace, top-left contextual AI core with
integrated expansion controls, SNAP/SHIFT, Simple/Advanced modes, customizable
collapsible panels, and independent strategy/robot entry points.

Keep the learner profile, credentials, user uploads, and unreviewed CAD assets
out of commits. Preserve third-party notices and licenses. Never author the
learner's challenge submission answers or publish/submit without authorization.
