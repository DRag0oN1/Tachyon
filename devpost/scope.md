# Tachyon

A new mobile-first AI robotics design application that turns competition rules
into understandable strategies and inspectable, realistic VEX robot assemblies.

## The Unique Kernel

The strategy explains the CAD: selecting an operating step reveals the actual
mechanism that performs it, and a supported AI change updates the assembly.
Competition interpretation, robot design, and mechanism explanation share one
coherent workflow.

## Who It's For

A VEX team member standing beside a robot with a phone, trying to turn game rules
into a buildable direction and understand how its mechanisms support a strategy.
Simple Mode guides beginners; Advanced Mode exposes more engineering detail.

## The Core Loop

Choose default Override or upload a supported competition rulebook. Start with
**Generate Strategy** or independently with **Create Robot**. Review grounded
objectives and execution steps, compare two mechanically distinct candidate
architectures, and inspect their connected component assemblies. Select a strategy
step to highlight its mechanism, understand its operation, and request a supported
change through the contextual AI core. Inspect the changed design and iterate.

## Inspiration & Identity

**Complicatedly Simple:** a sophisticated engineering workspace with clear,
contextual controls. **SNAP** uses refined LEGO Technic-inspired modularity;
**SHIFT** uses restrained Transformers-inspired mechanical motion. Both share
the same engineering data and navigation.

The CAD fills the workspace. A small animated top-left AI core expands through
controls inside its own bubble into a panel or full-screen view. Panels collapse,
mobile uses touch-friendly sheets, and Simple/Advanced modes and adjustable
layouts preserve focus. Support reduced motion and retain workspace preferences.

Product references: [VEX V5 competition](https://www.vexrobotics.com/v5/competition),
[LEGO Technic](https://www.lego.com/themes/technic), and
[Transformers](https://transformers.hasbro.com/). These are inspiration and subject
references, not permissions to copy branding or assets.

## Why This Matters to the Learner

Build a coherent, demonstrably working robotics design application that remains
useful beyond the challenge. A small set of supported mechanisms that genuinely
works is preferable to a broad set of simulated capabilities or decorative CAD.

## What "Working" Looks Like

In a working demonstration, the user selects Override, generates distinct
competition-aware strategies and two robot architectures, and opens a complete
conceptual robot assembly made from authentic VEX component geometry. Selecting
an acquisition or scoring step highlights the relevant real subsystem and shows
its explanation, annotated sketch, and field playbook. A supported AI request
changes the structured assembly and its visible geometry.

Another supported rulebook is processed through the same competition pipeline
with document references and explicit uncertainty. Both themes and mobile/desktop
workspaces work. Builds, runtime interactions, and failure cases are verified;
the demonstration video and public repository accurately show the implemented
functionality. No claim of physical validation or competition certification is
implied by a conceptual design.

## The POC Boundary

Build Tachyon's own focused pipeline: **PDF parsing → structured competition
model → LLM strategies/architectures → VEX component selection → assembly
construction → interactive 3D rendering**.

The proof of concept includes source-linked competition facts; two distinct
strategies/architectures; a small supported catalog of appropriately licensed
authentic parts; complete connected conceptual robot designs with component,
joint, and assembly identity; strategy-to-mechanism inspection with explanations,
sketches, and field playbooks; and a limited set of functional contextual edits.
Preserve independent entry points, the full-screen CAD workspace, three AI core
states, SNAP/SHIFT, Simple/Advanced modes, and collapsible/customizable layouts.

The exact supported mechanisms and modifications will be made explicit in the
PRD and technical specification. Component source rights and availability are
dependencies to resolve before claiming an authentic-CAD generation path works.
Missing parts and unsupported requests must remain visible. Source meshes are
display geometry and must not be described as editable solids.

Use fast build mode with the required planning approvals, testing, runtime
verification, useful early feedback, final review, and learning wrap-up/app map.
The submission describes the new application and records third-party attribution.

## Later

Expand the mechanism and part catalog; broaden supported competitions and edits;
add user CAD/sketch imports, direct assembly editing, reliable voice input,
Fusion/external CAD connections, and advanced simulation. Preserve extension
points and use truthful availability labels until each capability works.

## Explicitly Cut

- A massive multi-agent robotics framework: the focused pipeline proves the core
  experience without rebuilding a general research platform.
- Detailed physics simulation and optimization as hackathon deliverables: they
  would displace complete assemblies and the connected strategy/edit workflow.
- Generic primitive models labeled as authentic VEX parts, fake scoring or
  simulation results, text-only CAD changes, and enabled controls for unfinished
  features: these cannot demonstrate the product's claimed functionality.
